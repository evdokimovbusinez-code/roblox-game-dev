HOW TO INSTALL THE ROBLOX GAME DEVELOPMENT VAULT
================================================

Takes about 5 minutes. Free. Works on Windows, Mac and Linux.


STEP 1 - Get Obsidian (free)
   Go to https://obsidian.md, download it, and install it like any other app.


STEP 2 - Unzip the vault
   Windows: right-click the zip > "Extract All..." > choose a short place,
            for example C:\Vaults  (long folder paths can break the unzip).
   Mac:     double-click the zip.

   Which zip?
   - Lite (small): all the notes. Pick this if you are not sure.
   - Full (about 43 GB): the notes plus free textures, HDRIs and 3D models.
     Needs about 90 GB of free space while it unzips.


STEP 3 - Open it in Obsidian
   1. Open Obsidian.
   2. Click "Open folder as vault".
   3. Pick the folder you just unzipped (the one with "00 Start Here.md" inside).
   4. Obsidian asks "Do you trust the author of this vault?"
      Click "Trust author and enable plugins" to get the animated sky and welcome page.
      (Restricted mode works too. You just get a plainer look.)


STEP 4 - Start reading
   Open "00 Start Here". It has a map of every section and reading paths for
   beginners, scripters, artists, animators and designers.

   Handy keys:  Ctrl+O        find a note by name   (Cmd+O on Mac)
                Ctrl+Shift+F  search everything     (Cmd+Shift+F on Mac)
                Ctrl+G        open the graph        (Cmd+G on Mac)


THAT'S IT.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Something went wrong?
   - "Path too long" or missing files when unzipping: unzip to C:\Vaults instead.
   - The first open feels slow: Obsidian is indexing about 800 notes. Wait a minute.
   - No colours or sky: Settings > Community plugins > turn on community plugins,
     then enable "Celestial Vault".
   - Opened the wrong folder: pick the folder that contains "00 Start Here.md",
     not the folder above it.

Optional: using it with AI (Claude Code, Cursor and others)
   Open this folder in your AI tool. It finds AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md by itself.
   More detail is in "READ ME FIRST.txt".
