# LihFolk Vault V1: Roblox Game Development

An Obsidian vault that teaches every aspect of making games in Roblox Studio, in depth: Studio and workflow, Luau, architecture and networking, data, UI, world building, lighting/VFX/audio, animation, gameplay systems, game design, monetization and live ops, performance and testing, production, 3D modeling, AI tools, MCP and AI agents, pipelines, anti-exploit and security, debugging, worked builds, physics and vehicles, community and moderation, marketing, business and careers, a Luau cookbook, AI skills, case studies of top Roblox games, level design, procedural generation, advanced Luau, narrative, avatars and UGC, live ops, Studio plugins, game math, multiplayer services, accessibility, genre systems end to end, materials and surfaces, procedural Blender asset building, a 3,600-link resource library, game feel, economy design, Roblox platform features, team collaboration, 2D game development, a core concepts index, and modelling deep dives on voxel art, untextured stylised art and modelling readability. About 810 notes and 1,835,000 words, plus an asset library: 9 skeleton rigs (.fbx/.glb/.blend), 10 4K concept sheets, 869 CC0 PBR texture sets (2K) and 997 CC0 HDRIs (4K) and 520 CC0 models from Poly Haven, and all 50 CC0 Kenney 3D kits.

## Use it
- **As its own vault:** unzip it, then in Obsidian choose *Open folder as vault* and pick the `LihFolk Vault V1` folder. The first open indexes thousands of image files and can take a minute.
- **Inside your vault:** copy the folder into your vault. Links use note names, so they keep working.

Read **Using This Vault in Obsidian** for search, speed and AI tips. Start with **00 Start Here**: it has reading paths for beginners, programmers, artists, animators, designers, AI-tool users and live-ops teams.

The notes are plain Markdown and read fine without Obsidian.

## Sources
- **Roblox docs:** official documentation, checked in October 2026. Roblox changes quickly, so recheck anything time-sensitive (prices, payouts, policies, betas).
- **Measured:** real numbers and lessons from one production game.
- **Rule of thumb:** craft experience.

Conflicts and unverified points are listed in **Open Questions - Whole Vault**. The code was reviewed by hand but not run in your place: test it before you ship.
